What we stand for

Clean air first

Zero tailpipe emissions and near-silent motors, so school gates, campuses and city centres get quieter and cleaner.

Safety by design

LFP chemistry with liquid thermal control, ADAS, EBS and 360° cameras. Passengers come before specifications.

Lower cost per km

Electricity replaces diesel, and fewer moving parts cut maintenance. Operators keep more of every route's margin.

Support that shows up

Local engineers, remote diagnostics and spare parts, plus white-label branding so the bus carries your name.
